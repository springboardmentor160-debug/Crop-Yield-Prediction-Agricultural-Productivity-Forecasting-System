# YieldSense AI — Live Demo Script (target: 5 minutes)

Per the Demo Guide: script every click, pre-fill every form, and open on
your most impressive screen — not the login page.

## Before you start (same day, same machine)
- [ ] Run the full click path below at least once, today, on the exact
      laptop/network/login you'll use live
- [ ] Pre-fill the prediction form fields — don't type live
- [ ] Have 1–2 farms with existing prediction history seeded (empty
      dashboards look unfinished)
- [ ] Backup video recorded and tested on another machine
- [ ] Fallback screenshots of each screen below saved in your slide deck

## Click path

**1. Open on: Analyst Dashboard (not login)**
> "This is YieldSense AI — a platform-wide view of every farm's
> predicted yield, risk, and productivity."
- Point at: total predictions, average yield, risk distribution chart

**2. Click: Farm Comparison**
> "Here every farm is ranked by productivity score — this is what an
> agricultural cooperative or extension officer would use to see which
> farms need attention."
- Point at: top-ranked farm, productivity score column

**3. Click: New Prediction (as Farmer view, or switch role if demoing both)**
> "Now the core flow — a farmer enters their farm's soil and weather
> data and gets a yield forecast."
- Submit the pre-filled form
- Point at: predicted yield, confidence score, weather/soil impact scores

**4. Point at: Recommendation + Risk panel on the same result**
> "The system doesn't just give a number — it translates that into
> plain advice and a risk level, so a non-technical farmer knows what
> to actually do."
- Read one recommendation tip and the risk badge out loud

**5. Click: Weather or Soil analytics page**
> "This is what feeds the prediction — live weather impact scoring and
> soil health scoring, both normalized to a 0–100 score."

**6. Close on: your strongest results screen**
> "That's the full loop — data in, forecast and advice out, in under a
> few seconds." [end demo, don't linger on settings/empty states]

## Timing checkpoints
- 0:00–1:00 — Dashboard + Farm Comparison
- 1:00–3:00 — New prediction, result, recommendation/risk
- 3:00–4:30 — Weather/soil analytics
- 4:30–5:00 — Close on strongest screen, transition to Q&A

## If something breaks live
Say what should be happening, stay calm, switch to the backup video or
screenshot. Don't debug in front of the panel.
